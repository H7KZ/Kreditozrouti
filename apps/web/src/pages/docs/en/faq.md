# Frequently asked questions

## Does this enroll me in courses?

No. Kreditožrouti is for planning. Register and enroll through [InSIS](https://insis.vse.cz), and check its current dates and requirements.

## How do I fix a timetable conflict?

Open the affected course and choose a different lecture, exercise, or seminar slot. The app shows time overlaps and short transfers between known campuses.

## Can exchange students use it?

Yes. The interface is available in English, and you can filter courses by teaching language. Confirm eligibility and enrollment requirements with VŠE.

## Is an account required?

No. The app is free to use without an account. Your draft timetable and setup choices are stored in your browser.

## Where does course information come from?

The app uses VŠE's public InSIS catalog. Updates may arrive after changes in InSIS, so verify details there before enrolling.
