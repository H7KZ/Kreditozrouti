# Terms of Use

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

## About this service

Kreditožrouti is a free, student-initiated web tool for VŠE Prague students. It is not an official VŠE application. It
has no affiliation with, and is not endorsed by, Prague University of Economics and Business (VŠE).

## Permitted use

You may use Kreditožrouti to:

- Browse and filter VŠE course and timetable data
- Plan draft schedules before official enrollment (zápisy)
- Check for timetable conflicts between courses
- Share or bookmark your planned schedule

## Important limitations

**Kreditožrouti is a planning tool only.** It does not submit registrations to InSIS. Any schedule you build here must
be registered manually through the official InSIS enrollment system during the designated enrollment period.

**Data may not be fully up to date.** Course data is scraped from InSIS on a scheduled basis. There may be a delay
between changes in InSIS and their appearance in Kreditožrouti. Always verify course details and availability in the
official InSIS system before enrolling.

**No guarantee of accuracy.** While we make every effort to keep data accurate, Kreditožrouti makes no warranty -
express or implied - as to the completeness, accuracy, or fitness for any particular purpose of the information
presented.

## Shared links and exports

A share link or calendar link lets anyone who holds it view the selected timetable, including lecturer names. Share it
only with people you trust. Links expire after 180 days without a view. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## Programmatic access

API key, and the MCP server is meant for AI assistants acting for an individual user. Automated access outside these
channels, such as scraping the site, is not permitted. Rate limits apply and access may be blocked to protect the
service.
