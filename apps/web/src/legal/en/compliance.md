# Service and university rules

_Updated September 24, 2026_

Kreditožrouti is an independent student project, not a VŠE service. It reads publicly accessible InSIS course pages and does not register students or change InSIS records. Students must confirm course details and enroll through InSIS.

## Data and privacy

The catalog includes lecturer names. The app also handles technical request data, optional free-text feedback, and analytics and error events. Publicly available names can still be personal data. See the [privacy policy](../../pages/docs/en/legal/privacy.md) for the information used and retention periods.

## Automated access to InSIS

The scraper uses rate limits, job deduplication, and scheduled runs to limit load. These safeguards do not establish permission to automate InSIS. This repository does not document approval from the InSIS Steering Committee. The applicable university rule and any required approval should be confirmed with VŠE before claiming compliance.

The app is hosted outside VŠE webhosting. This page describes the project's current operation and open approval question; it is not a certification of regulatory compliance.
