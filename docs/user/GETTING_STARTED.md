# Getting started

1. Open Kreditožrouti and choose your faculty and entry year in the setup wizard.
2. Select the relevant study plan or plans. Mark completed courses if you want to hide them from browsing.
3. Filter the course list, expand a course, and select its lecture, exercise, or seminar slots.
4. Open **My Timetable** to check conflicts and compare saved schedules. Use the optimizer to explore alternatives.
5. Verify current course details and availability in InSIS before official enrollment.

Setup and timetable choices are stored in your browser. The app does not register you for courses. See the published [English](../../web/src/pages/docs/en/getting-started.md) or [Czech](../../web/src/pages/docs/cs/getting-started.md) guide for step-by-step help.
