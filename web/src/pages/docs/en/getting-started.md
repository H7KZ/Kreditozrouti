# Getting started

Kreditožrouti helps you explore VŠE courses and plan a timetable. It does not register you for courses. Complete official registration and enrollment in [InSIS](https://insis.vse.cz).

## Plan your timetable

1. **Choose your faculty and entry year.** The four-step setup wizard uses these to find relevant study plans.
2. **Select your study plan.** You can choose more than one where applicable.
3. **Mark completed courses.** This optional step helps hide courses you have already passed.
4. **Browse and filter.** Search by course name or code, or narrow the list by faculty, language, category, time, and other attributes. See [Filters](/docs/en/features/filters).
5. **Add teaching slots.** Expand a course, then select its lecture, exercise, or seminar slots. Choose each required unit type. See [Timetable](/docs/en/features/timetable).
6. **Resolve conflicts.** The app highlights time overlaps, short transfers between campuses, and missing unit types. You can compare saved schedules or use the [Optimizer](/docs/en/features/optimizer).

Your timetable and setup choices are saved in this browser. Check current course details and seat availability in InSIS before enrollment.

## Before you enroll

VŠE course groups, credit requirements, registration dates, and enrollment rules can vary by programme and year. Use your current study plan and the university's official guidance:

- [InSIS](https://insis.vse.cz) - course catalog and official enrollment
- [InSIS registration help](https://insis.vse.cz/help.pl?page=8968) - registration and enrollment instructions in Czech
- [VŠE IT Centre registration guide](https://ci.vse.cz/sluzby/dalsi/insis/jak-na-registrace-predmetu/) - practical guide in Czech
- [First-year InSIS help](https://insis.vse.cz/help.pl?page=14728) - starting with InSIS in Czech

Course times may span the Žižkov and Jižní Město campuses. Room codes beginning `RB`, `NB`, `IB`, or `SB` identify Žižkov in the app; `JM` identifies Jižní Město. Allow enough time to travel between them.
